What I do

Five ways in. Most engagements combine two or three.

The deal work comes first. Training is what clients buy second, once they’ve seen what happens to the deal.

Deal shaping & pitch

Take one must-win deal. Build the storyline, the business case and the executive pitch. Rehearse it with the team until it lands, and sit in the room when it’s told.

Sales team coaching

Live coaching on real deals, not role-play. Account planning, stakeholder mapping, and qualification that is honest about which deals are already dead.

Customer workshops

Facilitated executive sessions with your customer on vision, pain, roadmap and value. The session that moves a deal from interest to sponsorship.

Value selling training

Teach the team to quantify outcomes, build business cases that survive finance, and run the conversation in the customer’s language instead of the product’s.

Design thinking & ideation

When the use case isn’t obvious yet: structured ideation with the customer to find where AI creates real value, and how big the prize is.
